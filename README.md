SMART = Specific, Measurable, Assignable, Realist, Time-related
Let's create an interactive, collaborative, and innovative game together! ! On the Way to Mars.
Your goal is to manage and succeed in launching a space mission trip

The game helps bridge the gap between spatial concepts and technical decisions

* The content is drawn from resources provided by NASA and the CSA
* Concepts of resource management, decision-making, and exploration under time constraints
* Enabling players to learn through short games and by failing
* A detailed report at the end of the game
* Multiplayer available
